# 🌐 balaji4aws.github.io

The website serves as a portfolio of projects, technical articles, and engineering insights focused on solving complex business problems through Business Intelligence, Data Engineering, AWS, and AI.

## What you'll find

The website showcases:

- Business Intelligence solutions
- Data Products
- Decision Systems
- B2B Marketplace Analytics
- Supply Chain Analytics
- AWS Cloud Engineering
- AI & Intelligent Automation
- Engineering Articles
- System Design

Each project is presented through a business-first lens:

1. Business Problem
2. Context & Constraints
3. Solution Design
4. Technology Choices
5. Business Impact
6. Lessons Learned

The objective isn't to demonstrate technology alone, but to showcase how engineering, data, and AI put together can be applied to solve real business problems at scale.
